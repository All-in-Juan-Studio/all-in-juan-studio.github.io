  3. Your notes are yours. You own everything you write, draw and import. Tala Notes claims no rights to your content and only stores it so you can use it.
  4. Respect copyright. Only import or share books, pictures, PDFs and other material you have the right to use. If you think something in a shared notebook breaks your copyright or the law, tell us (see Contact below) and we will look into it and may remove it.
  5. Your account. Keep your password private. You're responsible for what happens in your account. Accounts that break these terms may be suspended or removed.
  6. Keep backups. Tala Notes is provided as is. We work hard to keep your notes safe, but we can't promise that data will never be lost, so make a backup now and then (Settings → Storage and backup).

  8. Changes. Features may change or be added, including optional paid features later. You'll always be told before anything costs money. If these terms change, the app shows you the new version.

  10. Law. These terms follow the laws of the Republic of the Philippines.

Privacy Policy

  • Without an account, your notebooks stay only on your device, in the app's own storage. Nothing is uploaded.
  • With an account, we keep only what sync needs: your email, an account ID, the sign-in dates Firebase records (if you sign in with Google, Firebase also keeps your Google account name and picture link), and your synced notebooks (handwriting, text, drawings, page settings, small cover pictures, stickers you make, your formula sheet and flashcard review dates). Photos and PDF pages you put on pages, your profile photo and your profile details stay on your device. This is kept with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), on Google servers that may be outside the Philippines.
  • Why. Only to give you the features you ask for: keeping your notes in sync and letting you share notebooks. We rely on your consent and on providing the service you signed up for.
  • Shared notebooks. If you share or join a notebook, its pages (with pictures in a smaller size) and the name you set in the app are stored so everyone in that group can see and edit them. Your name is only sent when you share or join a notebook. Anyone signed in who has the notebook's code can join, so only give it to people you trust.
  • Who can see it. Only you, plus the people in notebooks you share. As the app's administrator, All in Juan Studio can technically access stored data. It is only looked at to fix a problem or when you ask for help. It is never sold, rented or used for ads.
  • Handwriting reading. In the Android app, handwriting is read on your device with Google ML Kit (a language model is downloaded once). In a web browser that can't read handwriting itself, the app asks you first, then sends only the strokes you picked to Google's handwriting service. Nothing else is sent.
  • Other services the app uses. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you use an account, export a PDF or open a PDF, the app downloads helper code from Google, jsDelivr or cdnjs (Cloudflare). Like any website, these services can see your device's internet address when that happens. Fonts are built into the app, so no font requests leave your device.
  • No ads, no tracking, no cookies for tracking. Tala Notes has no ads and no analytics. The app saves your notebooks and settings in your device's storage, and Firebase keeps a sign-in token there so you stay signed in. Passwords are handled by Firebase and are never visible to anyone.
  • How it's protected. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS), and Google encrypts stored data. Access rules let only you, or the people in a shared notebook, read it. Content from other people's devices and from imported files is checked before the app uses it. No system is perfectly secure, so keep backups and use a strong password.
  • How long it's kept. Synced data stays until you delete it or your account. Things you delete in the app go to Trash for 30 days, then are removed.
  • If something goes wrong. If a security breach puts your personal data at real risk of harm, we will tell you and the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours of finding out, as the Data Privacy Act requires.
  • Your rights. Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173) you can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, and to withdraw your consent. Download a full copy any time in Settings → Storage and backup. Delete your account and its cloud copy in Settings → Account and sync, or on the web at all-in-juan-studio.github.io/delete.html. Deleting removes your account, your synced notebooks and your place in shared notebooks right away. You can also complain to the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.
  • Children. Tala Notes is not made for children under 13. If you're 13 to 17, please ask a parent or guardian before making an account.
  • Contact. For privacy requests, copyright reports or questions, email talasupportservices@gmail.com or use Send feedback in the app's Settings.
