Terms of Use

  1. Using Tala Notes. Tala Notes is a free notebook app made by All in Juan Studio for writing, studying and drawing. Use it for lawful purposes only. Don't use it to store or share anything illegal or to harm other people.
  2. Your notes are yours. You own everything you write, draw and import. Tala Notes doesn't claim any rights to your content and only stores it so you can use it.
  3. Your account. Keep your password private. You're responsible for what happens in your account. Accounts that abuse the service may be suspended.
  4. Keep backups. Tala Notes is provided as is. We work hard to keep your notes safe, but we can't promise that data will never be lost, so make a backup now and then (Settings → Storage and backup).
  5. Changes. Features may change or be added, including optional paid features later. You'll always be told before anything costs money. If these terms change, the app will show you the new version.
  6. Limits. As far as the law allows, All in Juan Studio isn't liable for lost data or other losses from using the app.

Privacy Policy

  • Without an account, your notebooks stay only on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • With an account, we store your email, an account ID, sign-in dates, the name you set in the app, and your synced notebooks and pages (handwriting, text and drawings). Pictures and PDF pages stay on your device and are not uploaded. Everything is kept with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) on servers in Singapore.
  • Shared notebooks. If you share a notebook or join one, its pages (with pictures in a smaller size) and the names of the people in it are stored so everyone in that group can see and edit them. Anyone with the notebook's code who is signed in can join, so only give it to people you trust.
  • Who can see it. Only you, through your account, plus the people in notebooks you share. As the app's administrator, All in Juan Studio can technically access stored data. It is only looked at to fix a problem or when you ask for help, and it's never sold or shared.
  • Backups. Backups you make (for example to Google Drive) are saved where you choose and are under your control.
  • Handwriting reading. In the Android app, handwriting is read on your device with Google ML Kit (a language model is downloaded once). In a web browser, when the device can't read handwriting itself, the strokes you selected are sent to Google's handwriting service to be read. Nothing else is sent.
  • No ads, no tracking. Tala Notes has no ads or analytics, and passwords are never visible to anyone.
  • Your rights. Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, you can ask to see, correct or delete your data. You can delete your account and its cloud copy any time in Settings → Account and sync, or on the web at all-in-juan-studio.github.io/delete.html. Deleting removes your account, your synced notebooks and your place in shared notebooks right away.
  • Under 18? Please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.
  • Questions? Use Settings → Send feedback.

Want to delete your account? Delete your Tala Notes account.
