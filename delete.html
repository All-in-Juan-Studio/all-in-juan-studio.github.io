Delete your Tala Notes account

You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Account and sync → Delete account) or here.

What is deleted: your account (email and sign-in), all notebooks and pages synced to your account, and your place in any shared notebooks. Shared notebooks you created stop being shared. This happens right away and can't be undone.

What stays: notebooks saved on your own phone or tablet stay there until you uninstall the app, and backup files you saved stay where you put them.
